\chapter{Evaluation and results}
\label{chap:evaluation}

This chapter of the report presents the evaluation of the performance and the cost-effectiveness of each of the modules of the proposed system.
The evaluation is based on the results obtained from the experiments and tests conducted on the system, and the analysis of the data collected during the experiments.
These results are compared against the initial aims, objectives, and requirements of the system, in order to determine the extent to which the system meets the desired performance and cost-effectiveness criteria.
Finally, they are compared against the results of similar systems in the literature, in order to assess the relative performance and cost-effectiveness of the proposed system.
