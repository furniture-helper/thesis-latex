\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}

This report presents a comprehensive framework for price tracking and product matching in Sri Lankan e-commerce.
The framework is designed to efficiently crawl multiple e-commerce platforms, extract relevant product information, and track price changes over time.
It also allows for the identification of similar products across different platforms, enabling users to compare prices and make informed purchasing decisions.
It leverages advanced machine learning techniques for page classification, information extraction, and product matching, ensuring high accuracy and performance.

The overall framework consists of seven primary modules:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item Distributed web crawlers
    \item Boilerplate removal and HTML minimization
    \item Data labeller
    \item Page classification
    \item Information extraction
    \item Product matching
    \item Proof-of-concept system
\end{enumerate}

These modules, all hosted in Amazon Web Services (AWS), work together to provide a comprehensive solution for price tracking and product matching in Sri Lankan e-commerce.
The final system was able to track over \texttt{73,000} products across \texttt{36} e-commerce platforms, and was able to provide users with accurate and up-to-date information on product prices and availability, with each product's price history being updated every 3 days.

This project empowers consumers in Sri Lanka to make informed purchasing decisions by providing them with the ability to track prices and compare products across different e-commerce platforms and helps tackle major issues in the Sri Lankan e-commerce market, such as the lack of price transparency and prevalence of price discrimination and fake discounts.
It also opens up opportunities for further research and development in the field of e-commerce analytics, particularly in the context of emerging markets like Sri Lanka.
